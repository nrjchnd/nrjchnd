I build systems at the intersection of **telecom, infrastructure, real-time media, and AI**.

My public work spans:
- **carrier and telephony systems**
- **real-time transport and communications infrastructure**
- **AI agents and automation platforms**
- **privacy-aware data processing and voice workflows**
- **product engineering from prototype to platform**

I like building things that are technically hard, operationally useful, and commercially real.

## Vision

I’m interested in the next generation of software where:
- **communications become programmable intelligence**
- **voice and media systems become AI-native**
- **infrastructure becomes autonomous, observable, and adaptive**
- **products are designed with privacy and scale in mind from day one**

I’m especially drawn to products that combine **deep systems engineering** with **practical business value** — the kind of software that can move from idea to deployment and actually survive contact with production.
